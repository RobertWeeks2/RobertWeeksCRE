Ground-Up Industrial Development

Deer Valley Industrial

Phoenix, Arizona | 80,000 SF

Project Overview

Deer Valley Industrial is an 80,000-square-foot ground-up industrial development underwriting case study in North Phoenix, Arizona.

The analysis evaluates land acquisition, development feasibility, construction costs, leasing assumptions, debt financing, stabilized operations and disposition proceeds.

The underwriting incorporates a monthly development cash flow model, comparable property analysis, construction and lease-up schedules, and a five-tier partnership distribution waterfall with a GP catch-up provision to evaluate project-level and investor-level returns.
